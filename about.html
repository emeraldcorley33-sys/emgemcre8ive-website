About Emerald

Arizona designer, custom design studio owner, and creator of joyful, intentional designs for Maricopa and Phoenix-area celebrations.

Hi, I'm Emerald.

I’m a designer, a mom of five, and a celebration‑obsessed creative based in Maricopa, AZ — where family, community, and color inspire everything I make.

I run a small but mighty design studio here in Maricopa, AZ, serving families, teachers, and small businesses across the Phoenix area. I believe every celebration, brand, and keepsake deserves to feel warm, polished, and intentional. Whether it's a birthday party, a brand refresh, or a personalized gift, I create designs that make moments memorable for Phoenix-area clients and beyond.

My approach

  • Clear communication: I listen to your vision and ask the right questions to bring it to life.
  • Fast turnarounds: Printables in 1-2 days, custom projects on a timeline that works for you.
  • Production-ready files: Everything ships print-ready so you can launch with confidence.
  • Strategy-led branding: Built for founders, marketers, and emerging brands who want their identity to stand out.

What I create

  • Branding suites with logos, color palettes, and style guidelines
  • Party packages for birthdays, baby showers, and holidays
  • Printables and keepsakes like wall décor, stickers, shirts, gifts, and memory pieces
  • Custom one‑of‑a‑kind designs for anything you dream up

Why I do this

EmGemCre8ive started with small projects for friends, teachers, and local families, and quickly grew as people kept coming back for designs that felt personal and joyful. What began as a creative outlet turned into a way for me to help others celebrate their biggest moments with intention.

I love helping people celebrate, launch businesses, and create keepsakes they'll treasure. Design isn't just about how something looks—it's about how it makes you feel. Every project I take on is a chance to add a little more joy and intention to someone's life.
